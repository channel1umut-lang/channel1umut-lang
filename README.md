### [Water Potability Prediction](https://github.com/channel1umut-lang/Machine-Learning-Applications#-final-project-water-potability-prediction): end-to-end ML project
Predicting whether water is safe to drink from 9 chemical measurements (3,276 samples).
- Train/test split **before** preprocessing to avoid data leakage; median imputation and scaling fitted on training data only
- 4 models tuned with 5-fold cross-validation: KNN, Decision Tree, Logistic Regression, **Random Forest (best, 0.67 accuracy)**
